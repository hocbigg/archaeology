# Advanced Topics in Archaeology

Learners who have finished the core undergraduate curriculum should pick one or two specialization tracks that fit their research interests or career goals, rather than working through every topic in order.

- Digital Archaeology & Computational Methods: Spatial analysis, 3D visualization, satellite remote sensing, and simulation modeling for investigating landscapes and sites.
- Environmental Archaeology: Scientific methods for analyzing animal bones, plant macroremains, geoarchaeological sediments, and isotopic signatures to reconstruct ancient ecosystems and diets.
- Bioarchaeology & Human Osteoarchaeology: Skeletal biology, paleopathological diagnosis, ancient DNA, and the mortuary analysis of human remains.
- Public Archaeology & Cultural Heritage Management: Regulatory compliance, Section 106, NAGPRA and repatriation ethics, museum curation, and site protection in contested environments.
- Material Culture Analysis: Laboratory characterization, compositional science, and experimental replication of lithics, ceramics, and ancient metallurgical technologies.

## Digital Archaeology & Computational Methods

Advanced computational and spatial tools for recording, modeling, and interpreting archaeological landscapes and material data.

Archaeological Spatial Analysis: [Archaeological Spatial Analysis: A Methodological Guide (Mark Gillings, Piraye Hacıgüzeller, & Gary Lock, eds.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780815373230). A comprehensive guide to spatial theory, point pattern analysis, viewsheds, and landscape modeling, written for archaeological questions.

Reproducible Computational Workflows: [The Open Digital Archaeology Textbook Environment (Shawn Graham et al.)](https://o-date.github.io). An open-access interactive text with executable Python and R notebooks on network analysis, text mining, and digital repository curation.

Agent-Based Simulation: [Agent-Based Modeling for Archaeology: Simulating the Complexity of Societies (Santa Fe Institute Press / Iza Romanowska, Colin D. Wren, & Stefani A. Crabtree)](https://santafeinstitute.github.io/ABMA/). A free, complete textbook with downloadable NetLogo code. It gives hands-on practice simulating ancient exchange, movement, and resource use.

Satellite Remote Sensing: [Advanced Archaeological Remote Sensing: Site Prospection, Landscape Archaeology and Heritage Protection (FutureLearn / Durham University)](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/remote-sensing-in-archaeology). A multi-week open course on multispectral satellite data processing, digital elevation modeling, and QGIS integration for site prospection.

Airborne Laser Scanning (LiDAR): [Guidelines for the Use of Airborne Laser Scanning (LiDAR) in Archaeology (EAC Guidelines 10 / Rebecca Bennett & Dave Cowley, eds.)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14609210). The European Archaeological Council's technical manual on point-cloud processing, digital terrain models, and visualizing landscape features.

## Environmental Archaeology

Scientific methods for analyzing ecological remains to reconstruct ancient climate, subsistence strategies, and human-driven environmental change.

Zooarchaeology: [Zooarchaeology (Elizabeth J. Reitz & Elizabeth S. Wing / Cambridge University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780521673938). The main manual for identifying vertebrate skeletal elements, calculating relative taxonomic abundance (NISP and MNI), and interpreting butchery and taphonomy.

Archaeobotany: [Archaeobotanical Online Tutorial (University of Sheffield)](https://sites.google.com/sheffield.ac.uk/archaeobotany/). An open practical tutorial on recovering and anatomically identifying charred seeds, fruits, and wood charcoal, and on interpreting them for diet.

Isotopic Analysis & Paleodiet: [Archaeological Science: An Introduction (Michael P. Richards & Kate Britton, eds. / Cambridge University Press)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780521144124). A textbook with in-depth chapters on carbon, nitrogen, strontium, and oxygen isotope ratios, used to trace ancient food webs, weaning patterns, and residential mobility.

Geoarchaeology & Site Formation: [Geoarchaeology: Using Earth Sciences to Understand the Archaeological Record (Historic England)](https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/geoarchaeology-earth-sciences-to-understand-archaeological-record/). An open technical handbook on sedimentology, soil micromorphology, and the depositional processes that create and modify archaeological sites.

## Bioarchaeology & Human Osteoarchaeology

The analysis of human skeletal and dental remains to reconstruct demographic profiles, morbidity, physical activity, and ancient population movement.

Human Osteoarchaeology: [Osteoarchaeology: The Truth in Our Bones (Coursera / Leiden University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/truthinourbones-osteoarchaeology-archaeology). A university-level online course on sex estimation, age-at-death assessment, skeletal biomechanics, and paleodemography. Free to audit.

Paleopathology: [A Concise Guide to Differential Diagnosis in Palaeopathology (Taylor & Francis / Efthymia Nikita & Mahmoud Mardini)](https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/oa-mono/10.4324/9781003475026/concise-guide-differential-diagnosis-palaeopathology-efthymia-nikita-mahmoud-mardini). An open-access diagnostic guide organized by disease etiology. It helps you evaluate pathological lesions, metabolic conditions, trauma, and infectious diseases on human bones.

Archaeogenetics & Ancient DNA: [Who We Are and How We Got Here: Ancient DNA and the New Science of the Human Past (Google Books / David Reich)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198821267). A foundational text on how paleogenomics isolates ancient DNA to trace migration, archaic introgression, and deep population history alongside the archaeological record.

Mortuary Skeletal Analysis: [The Archaeology of Human Bones (Simon Mays / Routledge)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138045675). The standard comprehensive text, combining anatomical analysis with archaeological context, burial taphonomy, and historical biological questions.

## Public Archaeology & Cultural Heritage Management

The legal frameworks, ethical mandates, professional standards, and community partnerships that govern archaeological practice and cultural heritage preservation.

Public & Community Archaeology: [Key Concepts in Public Archaeology (UCL Press / Gabriel Moshenska, ed.)](https://doi.org/10.14324/111.9781911576419). A peer-reviewed, open-access textbook on descendant community engagement, the politics of heritage, the illicit antiquities trade, and open scholarship.

Cultural Resource Management (CRM): [Cultural Resource Management: A Collaborative Primer for Archaeologists (Google Books / Thomas F. King, ed.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781789206524). A practitioner's guide to compliance archaeology, Section 106 review, adverse effect mitigation, and public preservation policy.

Repatriation & Indigenous Collaboration: [Plundered Skulls and Stolen Spirits: Inside the Fight to Reclaim Native America's Culture (Google Books / Chip Colwell)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226684444). A case-study analysis of NAGPRA compliance, museum curatorial dilemmas, sacred cultural property, and the ethics of collaborating with descendants.

Endangered Heritage & Protection: [Heritage under Threat (Coursera / Leiden University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/heritage-protection). An online course on heritage destruction during armed conflicts, looting prevention, international protection treaties, and crisis response. Free to audit.

Heritage Institution Management: [Arts and Heritage Management (Coursera / Bocconi University)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/arts-heritage). A structured course on governance, audience development, curatorial strategy, and financial sustainability for museums and archaeological monuments. Free to audit.

## Material Culture Analysis

Analytical, physical, and experimental approaches to reconstructing the manufacturing chains and operational sequences of ancient technologies.

Lithic Analysis: [Lithics: Macroscopic Approaches to Analysis (Google Books / William Andrefsky Jr.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780521615006). The standard laboratory manual on reduction sequences, core technologies, flake morphology, tool typology, and quantifying debitage attributes.

Ceramic Analysis: [Pottery Analysis: A Sourcebook (Google Books / Prudence M. Rice)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780226923215). The main reference on clay mineralogy, paste preparation, pyrotechnology, vessel morphology, and scientific provenance methods for archaeological ceramics.

Archaeometallurgy: [Archaeometallurgy in Global Perspective: Methods and Syntheses (Google Books / Benjamin W. Roberts & Christopher P. Thornton, eds.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781461490166). An advanced methods manual covering ore extraction, smelting slag analysis, metal alloy characterization, and elemental provenance.

Experimental Archaeology: [Designing Experimental Research in Archaeology: Examining Technology through Production and Use (Google Books / Jeffrey R. Ferguson, ed.)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781607320227). A guide to designing rigorous, repeatable experiments that test functional and technological hypotheses in material culture research.

Materials Science in Archaeology: [Materials in Human Experience (MIT OpenCourseWare / Heather Lechtman & Linn Hobbs)](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-094-materials-in-human-experience-spring-2004/). University lecture notes, readings, and lab modules that connect materials science, mechanical properties, and cultural choices in ancient ceramics, metals, and polymers.
